You are the **lead product architect, principal engineer, business-systems architect, and fintech/telecom platform engineer** for the existing **RoamLink** repository.

Repository:

`https://github.com/pectoraux/roamlink`

Your job is to transform the existing RoamLink codebase into the strongest commercially viable **connectivity platform** that can realistically be built on top of the existing architecture.

This is an existing product.

**Do not create a new application.**

---

# 0. THE BUSINESS THESIS

RoamLink is **not fundamentally an eSIM store**.

eSIMs are the initial customer-acquisition product.

The long-term business is:

# A UNIFIED CONNECTIVITY PLATFORM

RoamLink should eventually allow customers and organizations to acquire, manage, and pay for multiple forms of connectivity through one account:

* eSIM data
* regional/global data
* top-ups
* virtual phone numbers
* inbound SMS
* outbound SMS where commercially and technically supported
* voice/calling where commercially and technically supported
* recurring connectivity subscriptions
* connectivity bundles
* business/team connectivity
* future connectivity products

The customer should experience these as **one RoamLink connectivity relationship**, not disconnected products from different telecom suppliers.

The strategic principle is:

> RoamLink owns the customer relationship, product experience, pricing, billing, payments, distribution, analytics, support, risk management, and provider abstraction.

> Upstream connectivity providers supply the underlying telecom infrastructure.

Do not build telecom infrastructure unnecessarily when it can be obtained economically through legitimate provider APIs and commercial agreements.

# 7. AIRALO — CURRENT PROVIDER CONSTRAINTS

RoamLink currently has an Airalo reseller relationship.

Treat the following commercial facts as constraints:

* Airalo is currently an upstream provider.
* The relationship permits API-based resale of supported services.
* Current commercial terms include a postpaid purchasing model.
* Current approved credit limit is $10,000 USD.
* Current commercial pricing information indicates a 20% commercial term.
* Airalo invoices for prior purchases.
* Invoices are payable according to the contractual payment terms.
* Late-payment consequences can include interest and service suspension.
* Payment obligations survive termination.
* Taxes applicable to Airalo charges remain RoamLink's responsibility.
* RoamLink may not assume the credit facility represents cash or revenue.
* Current commercial information does not establish a top-up commission; never assume one exists.
* Provider pricing can change.
* RoamLink must maintain sufficient liquidity to settle provider liabilities.
* Current contractual restrictions regarding downstream resale must be respected.

Treat the actual signed/current agreement and official provider documentation as authoritative over this specification.

Never invent commercial terms.

Never infer undocumented provider capabilities.

Never represent the $10,000 credit facility as RoamLink cash.

# 8. PROVIDER CREDIT IS A FINANCIAL LIABILITY

The provider credit facility must become a first-class financial risk-control concept.

For every provider that extends credit, maintain:

* credit limit
* outstanding liability
* pending commitments
* available credit
* invoiced amount
* paid amount
* overdue amount
* projected liability
* utilization
* safety reserve
* payment due date

For Airalo:

```text
Credit Limit
-
Outstanding Liability
-
Pending Provider Commitments
=
Remaining Operational Capacity
```

Do not simply calculate available credit from completed orders.

Include known/predictable exposure where appropriate.

Create configurable thresholds:

```text
50%  informational
75%  warning
80%  elevated
90%  critical
95%  emergency
100% hard limit
```

Thresholds must be configurable.

At critical exposure, the system should proactively restrict or slow new provider commitments rather than waiting for provider rejection.

Administrators may override controls only through explicit, auditable actions.

# 9. CASH-FLOW SAFETY

RoamLink must understand that:

```text
Customer payment
≠
free operating cash
```

Some customer cash may effectively be required to settle:

* provider liabilities
* refunds
* chargebacks
* payment fees
* taxes
* operational expenses

Build visibility into:

```text
Customer cash collected
-
provider obligations
-
payment obligations
-
refund reserve
-
chargeback reserve
-
tax obligations
=
estimated available operating cash
```

This is a management metric, not necessarily a legal accounting statement.

Clearly label estimates versus finalized accounting values.

The system must make provider liabilities visible before management makes spending decisions.
